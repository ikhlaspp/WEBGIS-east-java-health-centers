# Regency and province aggregation — 2026-10-25

Shared module: python/gis_pipeline/analysis/aggregates.py. CLI: scripts/analysis/aggregates.py. It consumes a hash-pinned indicator artifact plus an independently hash-pinned administrative master. The master contains release_id, boundary_hash, regencies [{id,province_id}] and districts [{id,regency_id}]. District results must exactly match this master; missing/extra/duplicate identities, unknown or empty parents, mixed release/boundary, and marker filters are rejected. Each parent result lists exact district membership and data coverage by population, area, coordinate presence and eligibility.

P/F is sum(P)/sum(F) for complete population scope. P=30000+90000 and F=1+2 gives 40000. Aggregate deficit is the sum of district deficits; no recomputation of ceil(sum(P)/T). Missing P makes full totals, ratio and deficit null; the separately labeled complete-population subset has its own F. Coordinate quality is count weighted, not an average of district percentages. Missing coordinate counts are not zero. Marker service or eligibility filters cannot reduce administrative F. Area and fixed-scale ratio totals are retained with the output. Decimal values serialize at twelve places; source inputs are unchanged.
