## Facilities missing baseline coordinates

All 408 missing-coordinate records were searched in the frozen HDX Healthsites supplement using exact normalized names and a representative point inside the assigned historical district. Normalization removes case, punctuation and the whole words puskesmas, uptd and upt. There are 41 facilities with candidates, including one with multiple candidates; 367 have no match under these criteria. This does not mean no evidence exists elsewhere.

Polygon representative points are search aids only and must not be imported as facility coordinates. The supplement is dated 2026 and alone cannot establish a 2024 location. The private result retains facility identity, OSM identity, source feature hashes, and input hashes. Its hash is recorded in missing-location-discovery-summary.json.
